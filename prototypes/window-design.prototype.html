What does the ClipShift window actually look like?

Three structurally different answers, each rendered in every state a user can put it in. Flip variants with the bar at the bottom or the ← → keys. These disagree about what the window is for — a settings list, a picture of your desk, or a launcher — not about colour. Throwaway code; the winner gets rewritten properly.

On-screen indicator

Excluded from the recording itself, so this is purely about how much of the game it eats. Shown at true size over a 132 px-tall crop.

1 · Dot

9 px, corner, 75% opacity. Says “on”, nothing else. Cheapest to ignore, easiest to miss.

2 · Pill
14:32

Carries elapsed time, which is the one number worth knowing mid-session. Costs more pixels.

3 · Edge

A 2 px hairline along the top of the recorded display. Unmistakable, never over the action.
